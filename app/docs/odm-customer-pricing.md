# Customer-Specific ODM pricing

Pricing belongs to a `ProductSkuOdmCustomer` association. Each commercial revision is a `ProductSkuOdmCustomerPrice` row. A partial unique index permits one unarchived revision per SKU and Account. Editing terms archives the old revision and inserts a new one; removing an Account archives its association and active price. Existing Opportunity snapshots retain their values and the price revision remains referenced.

`customerPrice` is the active base price; `previousPrice` is reference history only. `tariffPercent` is stored as a percentage with four decimal places. When the amount is missing, tariff amount is `customerPrice × tariffPercent / 100`, rounded half up to two decimal places. When the percent is missing and base price is positive, it is `tariffAmount / customerPrice × 100`, rounded half up to four decimal places. With neither, both tariff fields are zero. Final Unit Price is base price plus tariff amount, with both money fields stored to two decimal places.

If a source supplies both tariff fields, the rounded calculation may differ from the supplied amount by at most $0.01. The supplied amount remains authoritative within that tolerance. A larger difference blocks the pricing row for review even if its Account is unresolved. Workbook `-` values remain unresolved source inputs; a blank or unavailable New Price stays in review until a usable price or historical-only disposition is chosen. The workbook importer writes customer pricing only after Customer-Specific classification and Account resolution. Its values never enter generic catalog tiers or Price Exceptions.

Gary's workbook can contain fractional cents from Excel formulas. The importer rounds workbook money values half up to cents and tariff percentages half up to four decimal places. It keeps the exact cell text in provenance alongside those operational values and warns when New Price changed by more than Excel's tiny binary representation noise.

Opportunity lines with `ODM_CUSTOMER` snapshot the Account, base price, tariff percent, tariff amount, final price, currency, and effective date. Editing that Opportunity keeps the snapshot if the same price revision remains selected, even when the current revision has since changed or been archived. Selecting a different revision creates a new snapshot. The Opportunity Unit Price must equal the selected final price; a different amount uses Manual pricing.

## September 2026 workbook semantics and review

The authoritative worksheet is `09.14.26` in `ODM customer pricing_Sep 2026.xlsx`. Row 2 is the header; the 121 populated rows are 3–123. [The row-by-row inventory](odm-workbook-sep-2026-inventory.md) records every source cell. The second `Old Price` column is the prior or updated quote, not another Old Price. The importer retains both values in source provenance; the first numeric Old Price is the operational `previousPrice`, falling back to the prior quote if Old is `N/A`, `-`, or blank. `New Price` is the candidate active customer price. Blank, `N/A`, and `-` are unavailable, never zero. CRM money is half-up cents while raw workbook decimal text remains in `sourceMetadata` and `OdmPricingImportSource`.

`Product` represents the model and its catalog category. `ProductSku` represents the exact part number and owns `catalogSource=ODM`, its valid `odmSubtype`, optional existing `baseSkuId`, and optional notes. `ProductSkuOdmCustomer` links a Customer-Specific SKU to an active Account. `ProductSkuOdmCustomerPrice` holds the active customer price, previous price, tariff, final unit price, notes, and workbook provenance. Customer-specific prices are not generic catalog tiers. `SPECIAL_CONFIGURATION`, `CABLE_PACKAGING_ACCESSORY`, and `OTHER` are valid ODM subtypes without an Account or active customer price. Base SKU is optional. A unique existing non-ODM SKU with the exact unsuffixed part number identifies an existing Product family and is recommended as the Base SKU, except when that unsuffixed SKU is also imported as ODM in the same workbook. A blank Base SKU never blocks import.

A Customer cell is an Account label, not proof of an Account relationship. A unique active Account name match or an existing SKU relationship with the same source customer label resolves automatically. In particular, `Amazon (thr BS -> Levata)`, `Zones & CDW`, `Genius / XENIAL`, and blank Customer cells are not silently mapped from a SKU suffix. Blank Customer rows 85–90 can be classified as non-customer-specific ODM or mapped to an Account deliberately. Rows with an active Customer-Specific price need an Account. Non-customer-specific and historical-only rows do not require Account resolution. The Account Resolution section groups source labels by normalized name, applies one selected Account to every affected entry, and keeps row-level overrides. Batch Account creation requires explicit confirmation, validates each proposed Account with the standard Account form rules, rejects CRM name duplicates, and creates the selected Accounts in one transaction. Composite customer labels are excluded from Select all safe candidates. Source text remains unchanged. The workbook does not reliably determine END_USER, OEM, VAR, or other CRM business roles, so role remains unclassified unless the source explicitly states one or an administrator selects it. Industry and Territory remain blank.

Structured tariff percent and amount control the calculation. Internally consistent structured tariff resolves automatically. Their mathematical disagreement requires review. A tariff mentioned only in Notes has no structured effect until selected; an unrelated price increase or discount percentage is not treated as a tariff. Notes containing a different price require a choice between the structured value and the first explicit note price; the whole note is preserved. Several NCR rows from 58 through 74 mention a 4.5% line while their structured tariff is 7.5%; row 72 also has a conflicting note price. Row 99 contains a post-discount note price. Row 3 describes a price increase without an added tariff line. Rows 6–17 include `N/A`, formula artifacts, and numeric notes. Rows 18–40 include 13.5% tariffs, missing New Prices, and accessory SKUs. Rows 53–57, 77, and 80 are discontinued. Row 85 has a new-part annotation that requires a corrected SKU. Row 66 contains two newline-separated SKUs and yields two entries that retain the same original cell. Rows 122–123 have external customer references `(Y...)`, which are kept in notes while the BIXOLON part is used as the SKU.

Each review entry is `READY`, `NEEDS REVIEW`, `ERROR`, or `SKIPPED`. Warnings alone do not block. The administrator flow is: apply recommendations; resolve or create Accounts; review and create the safe Product/SKU batch; decide grouped prices; decide grouped tariffs; review remaining exceptions; confirm import. The persistent progress summary refreshes after each preview resolution. Composite and annotated Account labels have a separate manual interpretation section with source text, row count, Account search, and an explicitly entered proposed name. They cannot enter the safe Account batch.

Apply Recommended Resolutions confirms deterministic subtype, Base SKU, and discontinued historical choices for the current preview session. Structured New Price is the active price candidate; Old and Prior remain provenance. Manual selectors can change recommendations. Price decisions are grouped only for identical source pricing and the same customer/SKU relationship, with Old, Prior, New, Notes price, proposed active price, and affected rows visible. Tariff authority decisions are grouped when the normalized customer, structured rate, Notes rate, and Notes pattern match; no Notes value is inferred automatically. A corrected active price requires an explicit administrator choice. The catalog section groups validated missing Products and SKUs, shows category, ODM classification, source SKUs and customers, existing Product matches, and affected rows. Administrators can select or deselect Products and SKUs, edit the proposed Product name, choose an existing Product, and edit subtype or Base SKU before confirming creation. This catalog-only step writes no pricing or customer links and immediately refreshes the current preview without re-upload. An existing SKU can be chosen by correcting the SKU in the entry review. Historical-only creates immutable source evidence without active pricing or catalog changes. This requires `OdmPricingImportSource`, the only new table: the existing price revision table cannot hold evidence for a row without a SKU and Account. An import transaction applies only Ready entries; source evidence is keyed by a content hash for idempotent re-import. Skip makes no database change. The migration must be deployed before using the revised importer; this change has not been deployed or applied to production data.
