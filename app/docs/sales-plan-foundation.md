# Sales Plan foundation

The official Sales Plan annual planned revenue total is the approved business commitment and annual quota for a rep, year, and currency. `SalesTarget` is the quarterly quota baseline used as the forecast coverage denominator. The rep-editable quarterly Sales Plan allocation records expected timing of business; it does not drive `SalesTarget`. Opportunity and Forecast represent current identified pursuits. Actual Revenue is reserved for a future external integration. Importing a plan never creates Opportunities.

An active Sales Plan has immutable annual line values. Admin and Sales Manager replace the official baseline by importing a higher revision; the previous revision becomes Superseded and remains queryable. A partial unique index allows one active revision per rep, year, and currency. The import fingerprint and revision identity reject re-import. The importer records source filename, hash, worksheet, row, source text, actor, and timestamps. Original Sales Rep, Account, and SKU text is retained on each line even when CRM records are linked.

Sales reps edit only their own active plan's `SalesPlanQuarterAllocation` records. Q1–Q4 units and revenue are nullable, nonnegative Decimal values. Annual values never change during allocation. Status is derived independently for each annual measure and requires every present measure to reconcile exactly. Partial and over allocations can be saved; over allocation is visibly flagged. Inactive or archived rep owners cannot allocate current plans.

The import UI analyzes XLSX sheets, then management chooses sheets, a header row and per-sheet column mapping, plan metadata, and a rep source: one selected rep per sheet or a Sales Rep column. Mappings can be saved and loaded for later workbooks; source row data and rep assignments are not saved in a mapping. The normalized preview shows exact source rows, warnings, CRM matches, and explicit resolution controls. Blank Account continuation rows show a suggested carry-forward, but remain unlinked unless explicitly approved. Unresolved Account and SKU text remains valid; the importer never creates catalog or CRM records. It re-parses and verifies the same workbook at finalization.

The inspected 2025 workbook has a consolidated sheet with Sales Lead, 2025 Units, and 2025 Revenue, plus rep-specific sheets and a Ryan media sheet. It also has a separate 2024 Nov–Dec sheet. The consolidated and rep sheets overlap; management must choose the intended source. Development Projects sections contain no genuine annual values and are excluded, as are totals. The inspected 2027 workbook has one consolidated-looking sheet but no rep column, a merged annual-plan heading, a row 3 header, one blank UPS continuation Account, no entered annual units or revenue, and comments with quarter hints. Its `Opportunity` column is Plan Item text; `2026 Revenue` is prior-year/reference revenue. Comments do not allocate quarters. Future templates are mapped by management rather than hardcoded.

Annual Target sums four active quarterly targets for the selected rep, year, and currency only when all four exist. Plan vs Target is unavailable if targets are incomplete. Quarterly Open Pipeline, Best Case, and Commit call the existing forecast service, which uses current operational Opportunities and Expected Close Date. No Opportunity is linked to a Sales Plan line and historical forecast values are not snapshotted. No FX conversion is performed.

Admin and Sales Manager may open **Sync Sales Targets** for one active rep from the Sales Plan page. The preview uses the full active official revision, including all lines with Annual Planned Revenue, independent of page filters and quarterly allocation. It shows current and proposed targets, annual totals, difference, and a derived status. Missing annual revenue and duplicate active targets block confirmation. The default is annual planned revenue divided by four using Decimal cents; any remainder goes to Q1, then Q2, then Q3, so the four quarters sum exactly to the approved annual plan. Confirmation creates missing quarters or reconciles existing active quarters in one serializable transaction. Archived targets remain untouched. A `SalesTargetSync` audit row stores actor, timestamp, source revision and annual amount, plus prior and resulting target IDs and values. The transaction rechecks the active revision and target snapshot; a changed plan or target requires another review.

Sync is an explicit management action. Imports, revision changes, allocations, and page loads never update targets. A newer active revision can leave existing targets out of sync until management confirms another sync. Sales and Read Only users can view comparisons but cannot sync; Marketing has no Sales Plan access. The management report derives sync status from current data. Existing forecast calculations continue to use `SalesTarget` without formula changes.

Admin and Sales Manager can import and view all plans. Sales can view their own plans and edit only their own quarterly allocation. Read Only can view globally. Marketing has no Sales Plan access. Server actions enforce each rule. The management report provides rep totals and Account/SKU line drill-down. Dashboard widget and Account detail card are deferred to keep the initial page and report bounded.
